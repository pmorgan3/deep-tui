# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the
community, and show empathy toward other contributors. Harassment, trolling,
personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban behavior they deem inappropriate. Report
conduct concerns privately to the security/contact address published by the
project maintainers once the public repository is created.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
